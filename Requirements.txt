Business questions you must answer by the end:

1. How is revenue trending, and what drives seasonality?
2. Which products, customers and countries generate most of the revenue?
3. How bad are returns and cancellations, and where do they concentrate?
4. Who are our best, at-risk and lost customers, and what should we do about each group?
5. Are customers coming back? How quickly does each acquisition cohort decay?
6. Which products are bought together, and how could that inform bundling?
7. What will monthly revenue look like over the next few months?


Tools

Python (pandas, NumPy, matplotlib/seaborn/plotly, scikit-learn, mlxtend, statsmodels or Prophet), 
SQL (SQLite, PostgreSQL or DuckDB), Power BI Desktop, Git/GitHub, and optionally Jupyter or Google Colab.
